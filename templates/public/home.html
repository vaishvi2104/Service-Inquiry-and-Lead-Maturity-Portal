Frequently Asked Questions

Essential procedural guidance regarding company incorporation, startup recognition, trademarks, and government seed fund applications.

A Private Limited Company is the standard choice for ventures intending to raise equity funding or scale with external investors. An LLP offers limited liability with fewer annual statutory filing compliances, making it suitable for professional partnerships and bootstrapped businesses. A One Person Company (OPC) allows a single entrepreneur to operate an incorporated entity with limited liability.

The entity must be registered as a Private Limited, Partnership, or LLP in India, incorporated within the last 10 years, have an annual turnover not exceeding INR 100 Crore in any financial year, and work towards innovation, development, or commercialization of products/services, or possess a scalable business model with high employment potential.

Trademarks in India are registered under 45 distinct classes (Classes 1–34 for goods, Classes 35–45 for services). Filing in the wrong class leaves your primary trade activities unprotected and may cause application abandonment or objection. Our advisory includes rigorous class mapping before filing.

We assist eligible DPIIT-recognized startups in preparing their business documentation, milestone plans, and compliance declarations required when applying through eligible incubators under the Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS). Fund sanctions are governed independently by incubator evaluation committees.
